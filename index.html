Architecture & Intelligence Pipeline

  • Stationarity Transformations: Augmented Dickey-Fuller (ADF) testing, logDifferencing, and rolling mean variance stabilization.
  • Econometric ARIMA: Optimal (p, d, q) hyperparameter discovery via Akaike Information Criterion (AIC) minimization.
  • Deep LSTM Neural Network: Recurrent architecture with Dropout regularization built with TensorFlow/Keras (`lstm_model.h5`).
  • Hybrid Volatility Ensemble: Combining linear econometric stability with non-linear deep learning trend prediction.
